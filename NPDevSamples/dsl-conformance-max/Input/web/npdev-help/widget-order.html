A widget order moves through its lifecycle one step at a time; each shipment event recorded against it feeds the "Shipped units by warehouse" donut beside this note.

  • Create the order, then add its lines.
  • Ship it: every shipment event adds units to its warehouse's slice.
  • Click a slice or legend entry to open the shipment list filtered to that warehouse.
